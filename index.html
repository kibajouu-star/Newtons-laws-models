Model 1: First Law, Inertia

An object stays at rest or continues moving at a constant velocity unless an unbalanced force acts on it.

→

When the outside force acting on the moving car is 0, it continues moving. You can try adding friction or adding more force to move the cart faster.

Model 2: Second Law — F = ma

An object's acceleration increases with force and decreases with mass.

→
Acceleration = force ÷ mass.

Model 3: Third Law — Action and Reaction

When two objects touch/collide, they exert equal and opposite forces on each other.

←
→
The forces are equal in size and opposite in direction.

Conclusion

First Law: Motion does not change unless a net force acts on an object.

Second Law: Acceleration depends on net force and mass. F = ma.

Third Law: Interaction forces are equal and opposite, but different masses can have different accelerations.

These are simplified models. Real carts may experience friction, gravity, etc.
